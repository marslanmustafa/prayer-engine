📖 Developer Integration Guide for @marslanmustafa/prayer-engine

Universal prayer times, Qibla compass, and Hijri moon-sighting engine with multi-school support. Zero external dependencies, TypeScript native, compatible with React Native, Next.js, and Node.js.

Bash (Installation) 
npm install @marslanmustafa/prayer-engine

Core Islamic Concepts Calculated

☀️ Dahwa-e-Kubra (ضحوہ کبریٰ)

The Islamic Midday. Exact halfway point between Subh-e-Sadiq (Fajr) and Maghrib: Fajr + (Maghrib - Fajr)/2. In Fiqh Hanafi, it is the strict deadline for Ramadan fasting intention (Niyyah).

⚠️ Makrooh Midday Window (Zawaal)

Voluntary prayers are prohibited during solar midday. The engine calculates the exact interval from Dahwa-e-Kubra to Dhuhr start.

⏱️ Precautionary Minutes (Ihtiat)

Traditional Islamic calendars add safety margins: -2 mins before Fajr to conclude Sehri safely, and +2 to +3 mins after sunset before breaking fast.

⛰️ Elevation Horizon Dip

Elevated cities (Lahore at 210m, Quetta at 1,680m) experience earlier sunrise and later sunset due to atmospheric horizon depression.

🌙 Ruet-e-Hilal Moon Engine

Allows [-2 to +2] day offsets to synchronize with local Ruet-e-Hilal committees, with Urdu/Arabic month names and Gyarween Sharif detection on the 11th.

🧭 Qibla Compass Bearing

Calculates exact compass bearing from any coordinates directly towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah.
